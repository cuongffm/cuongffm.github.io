Bob Uploader

Uses YouTube API Services

Bob Uploader is a private, non-public tool used only by the creator of the YouTube channel @bob_foundout. It uploads the creator's own, self-made animated Shorts to the creator's own YouTube channel, sets their thumbnail and schedules their publication.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos (videos.insert) to the owner's channel as scheduled private videos with a publish time
  • Sets the custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set)
  • Checks upload/processing status and avoids duplicate uploads (videos.list, channels.list, playlistItems.list)

It is not offered to other users, has no public sign-up, and never accesses any other account, channel or user data.
